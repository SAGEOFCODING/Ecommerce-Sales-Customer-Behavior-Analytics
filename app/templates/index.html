EDA Diagnostic Synthesis

Fulfillment & Reversal Leakage

Critical Severity
Identified Exposure 41.42% Reversal Rate ($523.9k GMV)
The Diagnostic Finding

Only 19.25% (231 orders) of shipments reach completion. A combined 41.42% (497 orders) are reversed via cancellations (20.83%) and customer returns (20.58%), indicating high pre-dispatch buyer remorse and post-delivery product expectation mismatches.

Strategic Action Plan (How to Fix)
  • Real-Time Delivery Telemetry: Deploy instant automated SMS/WhatsApp dispatch tracking notifications within 30 minutes of purchase to minimize pre-shipment buyer remorse cancellations.
  • High-Ticket Checkout Verification: Institute an OTP re-confirmation step for cart totals over $1,200 (Laptops & Tablets) to eliminate impulsive or accidental transactions before warehouse picking.
  • Pre-Return Exchange Incentives: Offer instant 110% store credit or warranty exchanges at the return request stage to prevent direct cash reversals.
Target: Reduce cancellations from 20.8% to <12%, recovering ~$110,000 GMV

Promotional Margin Erosion

High Priority
Identified Exposure 74.25% Discount Penetration (891 Orders)
The Diagnostic Finding

Promotional codes are utilized in nearly 3 out of every 4 transactions. While the arithmetic mean order value is $1,053.97, the median rests at $823.62, proving that blanket discount codes are heavily subsidizing sub-$800 purchases without expanding cart size.

Strategic Action Plan (How to Fix)
  • Minimum-Spend Thresholds: Eliminate unconditional discount codes. Implement tiered promotions (e.g., "$75 discount on orders exceeding $1,000") to lift smaller carts above the median.
  • Cross-Sell Bundle Requirements: Restrict high-demand hardware (Laptops/Tablets) from standalone discounting, requiring accessory bundling (Headphones/Keyboards) to unlock promotional savings.
  • Loyalty Point Accrual: Shift from upfront price discounts to post-delivery loyalty credits that incentivize high-margin repeat purchases.
Target: Lift median AOV from $823.62 toward $1,050+ while preserving operating margin

Channel Attribution & ROI

Growth Opportunity
Identified Exposure Instagram & Email: $537.1k Revenue (42.5%)
The Diagnostic Finding

Instagram ($275,285) and Email ($261,808) significantly outperform Google ($250.4k), Facebook ($250.4k), and Referral channels ($226.8k). Direct word-of-mouth referrals underperform by nearly $50,000 compared to social acquisition.

Strategic Action Plan (How to Fix)
  • Marketing Budget Reallocation: Shift 20% to 25% of ad spend from general Facebook display ads into high-converting Instagram visual storytelling and influencer integrations.
  • Automated Lifecycle Email Workflows: Capitalize on high email conversion by building 3-step automated cart abandonment and post-delivery replenishment sequences.
  • Two-Sided Referral Gamification: Revamp the referral program into a double-incentive model ($30 credit for the referrer + $30 discount for the friend) to drive organic advocacy.
Target: Grow referral revenue from $226.8k to >$260k with lower blended CAC
